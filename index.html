Possible urgent symptoms

What you described can be a warning sign. Please get care now instead of waiting.

  • Sudden loss of vision, or a dark curtain over part of your sight
  • Severe eye pain, or new flashes and many new floaters
  • A chemical in the eye, or an injury to the eye

Go to an emergency department or call your local emergency number.

Eye-care tips
    
      
  • Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
    • 
      
  • Blink often and keep screens slightly below eye level.
    • 
      
  • Wear sunglasses with UV protection outdoors.
    • 
      
  • Never rub your eyes. Wash hands before touching them or contact lenses.
    • 
      
  • Get a full eye exam regularly, and yearly if you have diabetes.

      

    
Finding the right doctor
    
      
  • Optometrist: vision tests, glasses, routine checks.
    • 
      
  • Ophthalmologist: eye diseases, surgery, urgent problems.
    • 
      
  • Bring your list of symptoms, medicines, and any earlier eye reports.
    • 
      
  • Sudden vision change, severe pain, or injury: go to an emergency department.
    • 
      
  • Search your local health directory or ask your insurer for in-network eye doctors.
